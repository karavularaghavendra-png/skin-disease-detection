⚡ Efficiency Metrics

🧪 Custom CNN

✅ Strengths

  • ✅ Lightweight and fast inference
  • ✅ Easy to understand and modify
  • ✅ Good for small datasets
  • ✅ Lower computational requirements

🚀 MobileNetV2 (Transfer Learning)

✅ Strengths

  • ✅ High accuracy from ImageNet pretrained features
  • ✅ Excellent generalization on small datasets
  • ✅ Fine-tuning unlocks domain-specific adaptation
  • ✅ State-of-the-art mobile-optimized architecture
💡

Recommendation: Use MobileNetV2 with Fine-Tuning for production. It achieves 10–15% higher accuracy than the Custom CNN by leveraging pretrained ImageNet features. The Custom CNN is suitable for learning purposes and resource-constrained environments where model size matters.
